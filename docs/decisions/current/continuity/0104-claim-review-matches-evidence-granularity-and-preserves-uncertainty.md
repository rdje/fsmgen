# 0104 — Claim review matches evidence granularity and preserves uncertainty

- Date: 2026-09-23
- Status: accepted
- Type: evidence architecture/governance
- Owner: `CLAIM-VERIFICATION-TEMPLATE-REVIEW.2`

## Context

The director-selected template changed after the adoption in `0074`. The exact
source, diff and selections are retained in `docs/audits/claim-template-review-20260923/`.
Review alone did not change policy; this revision deliberately applies it.

## Decision

Extend `0074` with domain-free rules, evidence at the claimed unit, scoped set
enumeration with classified hits, and comparison with prior adjudications.
Attribute changes through safe controlled comparisons; name a missing comparison
instead of replaying spent captures or bypassing admission. Assess challenged
prose, quantities and named instances separately under their recorded scope.
Fixed-input censuses and contract identities are exact; stochastic measurements
still need sampling and intervals. Reject an immediate unconditional affirmation
as a truth test. History can be corrected by evidence; newness proves nothing.
Preserve incidental-numeral exclusions, explicit owned gaps and structural-gate
limits and dependency black-box boundaries. No checker or HIAL admission change.

## Consequences

The root policy and mdBook carry the applied rules. Imported deployment figures
and another project's assent remain source history, not local measurements or
authority. The compacted `0074` retains its decisions and original context;
its exact pre-compaction text remains in Git. No byte/line allowance is raised.
