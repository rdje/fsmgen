FSMGen ships `ppif/axi_ar_driver.ppif`, parsed as additive
`(axi-ar-driver ...)` and generated by
`FSM::IAL2::ProtocolIntent::AxiArDriver`. The driver atomically captures one
idle address32/ID4/LEN8/SIZE3/BURST2 command, launches ARVALID independently of
ARREADY, holds the complete payload through stalls, and accepts exactly once.

The mandatory lowering path produces one `axi_ar_driver.isf`, one
`axi_ar_driver.fsm`, then HDL. Its schedule has 15 ports, six states, 8/3
launch/accept assignments, no issues, and three accept-over-launch priorities.
The report schema is `fsmgen.ial2.protocol_intent.axi_ar_driver.v1`; its
`request_scope` says `done_event = ar_request_accepted` and
`includes_read_response = false`.

The four-subtest `t/1504` proves strict/support/semantic/schedule/outdir,
Verilator and Yosys, targeted rejection cases, continuously-ready and delayed-
ready cardinality, payload stability while inputs mutate, busy-command ignore,
idle/active reset, post-reset recovery, exact 3/3 handshake/done totals, and
final idle. The primitive does not drive RREADY, capture RID/RDATA/RRESP/RLAST,
or claim full read completion.
