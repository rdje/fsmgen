<p>These should stay precise and sometimes narrower than the book.</p>
<p>The Knowledge Map checks each fact card against its configured line, encoded
byte and line-width bounds. Both generation and validation count the original
UTF-8 bytes; a non-ASCII character can occupy several bytes. Invalid UTF-8 is
rejected. Query-cache fingerprints include the complete generated shard bytes,
so a byte change invalidates a cached projection even when its questions stay
the same. The collection’s containment allowance is a separate gate; passing
one card’s bounds does not authorize collection growth. An authoring script’s
local precondition does not establish an enforcement limit: use the checker,
configuration and actual accepted and refused boundary cases. See
<a href="#knowledge-map-architecture">the Knowledge Map architecture</a>
and its public checker, <code>knowledge-map/scripts/check_knowledge_map.sh</code>.</p>
<p>A collection rollover keeps each reader question at a bounded current card and
provides authenticated retrieval of its complete dated predecessor. Current
rules use canonical doctrine and registries; historical measurements keep their
capture boundary. Preserving counts alone cannot prove that question ownership
or unique information survived a move.</p>
<p>For eligible transition debt, the declared allowance covers measured actual plus
one declared step, clamped at the enforcement ceiling, with a live owner. At
rollover debt, perform the declared rollover; widening its allowance is forbidden.
Card limits, aggregate limits and the transition allowance are independent.
A compliant line width or an unchanged question count does not establish whole
surface health or preserved question ownership.</p>
<p>The containment cards now separate current rules from complete dated sources.
Retrieve a predecessor by its existing card ID from the repository root:</p>
<pre><code class="language-sh">python3 -B docs/audits/knowledge-card-rollover-selection-20261005/read_source.py live-document-size-containment
</code></pre>
<p>The same reader accepts <code>live-document-surface-growth-procedure</code>,
<code>knowledge-card-sizing-and-partition</code> and
<code>live-document-transition-allowance-headroom</code>. It checks the complete retained
file digest before returning the original bytes. The doctrine gate also checks
these file archives. Earlier measurements and superseded instructions keep their
capture boundary; current writing follows the canonical doctrine and registries.</p>
<p>Captured metadata must retain its declared field kind and the recorded producer
form. A Boolean does not become an integer field because a host language compares
them equal. A public schema may admit equivalent integral numeric forms, while an
exact capture reader must still preserve the original producer representation.
Check these boundaries separately; a private comparison defect does not make
correctly captured source values wrong. Rollover records now use a superseding
reader that pins original source, programme, proof and receipt bytes to their
publication revision before checking their recorded metadata kinds.</p>
<p><code>CLAIM_VERIFICATION.md</code> defines what earns an actionable quantitative claim:
re-derive it from its source, attempt to falsify it with a separating oracle,
and keep a tracked producer plus a watcher that detects staleness. A missing
leg is published explicitly rather than hidden behind repeated checks of the
same kind. <code>scripts/check_claim_verification.pl</code> gates exact bounded records and
tracked local paths. Decision <code>0094</code> makes a finite manifest-addressed
registry set the repository default while retaining one global claim namespace;
explicit <code>--registry</code> remains for bounded single-file fixtures. The separately bounded current-surface inventory derives
its scope from the live-document registry and sends every open candidate to a
task owner. Its bounded disposition registry then joins each reviewed current
candidate to a published claim record, a three-leg derived gate, a reviewed
incidental reason, or an explicit owned gap; a completed source group cannot
retain an open candidate. Run the five-group report with
<code>scripts/check_claim_verification_dispositions.pl --report</code>. Decision <code>0074</code>
keeps semantic truth and oracle independence outside those structural gates’
claims.</p>
<p>The reviewed policy also requires evidence at the level being claimed. State
its unit, population, predicate and input revision. A suite or group result
cannot establish each member’s property without evidence connecting the two.
For an absence, uniqueness or completeness claim, retain the scoped enumeration
and its command. Classify search hits before calling them defects.</p>
<p>For example, finding a validation word in several tests identifies candidates
to inspect; it does not prove that each test checks the same behavior. Conversely,
finding no match for that word does not prove the behavior is untested. The
assertions and the public behavior they exercise provide the relevant evidence.
A claim naming one test must identify that test and its actual checked property.</p>
<p>Consult earlier adjudications before reporting a conflicting result, and name
any difference in source revision, contract, scope or evidence. An earlier ruling
can be wrong, but another search result alone does not overturn it. Attribute
a change through a safe controlled comparison or isolated reconstruction when
possible; preserve first-failure evidence, unrelated user work and existing
admission gates. A spent or prohibited capture stays prohibited. Publish a
missing attribution leg and its repair owner if the comparison is unavailable.</p>
<p>Review a challenged claim’s prose, quantity and named instance separately under
its recorded conditions. A fixed-input census should reproduce exactly; a
contract value or named identity requires an exact match. A runtime measurement
can vary even when a command produces it, so report its sampling method and
interval instead of implying precision from one run. Any judgment tolerance
must be justified before comparison. Honest rechecking and correction remain
part of verification; confidence alone is not evidence. Decision <code>0104</code> records
these adaptations, and incidental numerals keep their existing semantic
contracts without becoming claim records merely because they contain digits.</p>
<p>The regenerated inventory also consumes the canonical disposition identities:
closed candidates carry no residual migration owner, while a new candidate
remains visibly task-owned until it receives a disposition. A compact
original-cohort manifest names the exact adoption commit, then reconstructs
per-source identity sets from that versioned source and checks their counts and
digests. Current files must retain every original identity, but later numeric
fields remain current-only even when nested on a historical JSONL line. Current
derived constants retain re-derivation, falsification, and durability legs;
configured policy values and schema versions retain explicit input-identity,
falsification, and durability gates. Run the reconciliation report with
<code>scripts/check_claim_verification_inventory.pl --report</code>.</p>
<p>The first root-document review repaired stale README-policy wording that had
described ratcheted enforcement ceilings as still pinned to their adoption
baseline. Every current non-rationale root policy, index, navigation, and
structural candidate now resolves to either a live derived gate or a reviewed
policy, history, structure, or identifier disposition. The rationale-ledger
measurements are likewise closed by the evidence-coherent reviews below;
derive current counts with the report command above instead of copying them
into this inventoried surface.</p>
<p>The foundational rationale review separates live gates from immutable context.
Ledger reconstruction, the complete roadmap archive, and qualified provider
source ordering use executable producer, separating-oracle, and watcher chains.
Book-partition and earlier roadmap-occupancy statements remain explicitly
historical. Unsupported precision in an experimental host-resource observation
was removed while retaining the checked guarded-envelope outcome and
lower-memory compilation control.</p>
<p>The bridge and execution rationale review also keeps independently selected
scale axes independent in the evidence. Bridge source-map calibration is
reconstructed with differential real semantic builds, while exact boundary
cases retain their ordinary builder outcome. The earlier scenario-map collision
is historical context watched by the repaired global-map invariant. Total and
live fibers use distinct generated tree shapes and mutation oracles, and an
execution type counts only when a real public binding exercises its semantic

<div id="knowledge-map-architecture"></div>

<h1 id="knowledge-map-km--a-derived-question-keyed-retrieval-layer"><a class="header" href="#knowledge-map-km--a-derived-question-keyed-retrieval-layer">Knowledge Map (KM) — a derived, question-keyed retrieval layer</a></h1>
<p>A portable, <strong>harness-agnostic</strong> extension to the Durable Memory Architecture that makes
sure an AI/LLM (or a human) <strong>never has to do archaeology</strong> — re-deriving a fact from code
or runtime — to rediscover something that was <em>already logged once</em>.</p>
<p>This file is intentionally <strong>project-agnostic</strong>: it names no nouns from any one codebase.
Drop the <code>knowledge-map/</code> bundle into any repository that already uses <strong>task-trees</strong> +
<code>MEMORY_ARCHITECTURE.md</code> and it applies as-is.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>One-line thesis: <strong>durable facts become findable when each is a small, self-describing,
front-mattered file, and a machine derives a bounded question-keyed index plus
topic shards from them — so retrieval is one query, not an excavation.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="0-the-problem-this-solves-and-the-one-it-does-not"><a class="header" href="#0-the-problem-this-solves-and-the-one-it-does-not">0. The problem this solves (and the one it does not)</a></h2>
<p>Teams that already write things down still watch a fresh agent <strong>re-derive facts from
code</strong>. That is <em>archaeology</em>, and it is a <strong>retrieval</strong> failure, not a recording failure:
the fact existed (in a task leaf, a commit message, a decision record) but the next session
could not <em>find</em> it, so it dug it out of the source again and — worse — sometimes dressed
the re-derivation up as diligence.</p>
<p>Be honest about the ceiling. There are <strong>two</strong> kinds of “finding a fact”:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Re-deriving a fact that was already established</strong> (a root cause, an architectural
decision, a gotcha). This is the archaeology the KM <strong>eliminates</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Measuring the current state of a living system for the first time</strong> (today’s metric,
which case fails right now). You <em>cannot</em> pre-write a measurement you have not taken;
this is legitimate <strong>diagnostics</strong>, not a failure. The KM’s job there is narrow: once
measured, its durable <strong>conclusion</strong> becomes a fact card so nobody measures it twice.</li>
</ul>
<p>Target: <strong>zero archaeology for structural/causal facts; diagnostics only for genuinely-new
state, whose conclusions then become facts.</strong></p>
<hr>
<h2 id="1-why-a-derived-index-and-not-a-hand-curated-one"><a class="header" href="#1-why-a-derived-index-and-not-a-hand-curated-one">1. Why a derived index (and not a hand-curated one)</a></h2>
<p>A hand-maintained index is the very anti-pattern <code>MEMORY_ARCHITECTURE.md</code> forbids
(“prefer derived over hand-written … so it cannot drift”; “hand-maintained current-state
that drifts” is an explicit anti-pattern). It also costs time at every commit. So the KM
splits the two costs and keeps only the cheap one:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Curation cost</strong> — a human/agent deciding what to index and phrasing the keys. This is
the expensive, rot-prone part. The KM <strong>eliminates</strong> it: the keys live <em>inside</em> each
fact file, authored once, when you write the fact you were recording anyway.</li>
<li><strong>Generation cost</strong> — a script scanning files and emitting the map. The
<strong>machine</strong> pays it (in the pre-commit hook), never the workflow, never the agent.</li>
</ul>
<p>The map is a <strong>build artifact</strong>, never hand-edited, <strong>deterministic</strong> (sorted, no
timestamps) so “regenerate-and-diff” is a valid sync gate. Because the facts are atomic and
front-mattered, an agent can also <strong>skip the map entirely</strong> and grep the fact files
directly — the map is a convenience cache, not a load-bearing dependency. (Contrast a
hand-index, where staleness breaks everything.)</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="2-what-a-fact-is"><a class="header" href="#2-what-a-fact-is">2. What a fact is</a></h2>
<p>A <strong>fact</strong> is one <code>.md</code> file whose YAML front-matter has a <strong>non-empty <code>answers:</code> list</strong>.
The presence of <code>answers:</code> <em>is</em> the marker — no separate flag. Required and optional
fields:</p>
<div class="table-wrapper">
<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Field</th><th>Req?</th><th>Purpose</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><code>id</code></td><td>✅</td><td>stable, unique, kebab-case identifier (used as the map anchor)</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>title</code></td><td>✅</td><td>one-line human title</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>answers</code></td><td>✅</td><td>the <strong>questions a future agent will search for</strong>, in <em>their</em> words (this is the retrieval surface — list synonyms)</td></tr>

<tr><td><code>evidence</code> <em>or</em> <code>reverify</code></td><td>✅ (≥1)</td><td>what proves it / the cheap command to re-confirm it</td></tr>

<p>Front-matter format is a <strong>constrained YAML subset</strong> parsed without third-party
modules: scalars <code>key: value</code>; inline lists <code>key: [a, b]</code>; block lists (<code>key:</code>
then <code>  - item</code>); and folded <code>&gt;-</code> scalars for long <code>evidence</code>, <code>reverify</code>, or
<code>answer</code> values. Folded continuation lines use at least two spaces and are
joined with one space. Use <strong>plain or double-quoted</strong> scalars, never tabs;
questions containing a comma use the block-list form. Copy
<code>templates/FACT_TEMPLATE.md</code> to start.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="3-the-six-properties-llms-need-why-the-format-is-shaped-this-way"><a class="header" href="#3-the-six-properties-llms-need-why-the-format-is-shaped-this-way">3. The six properties LLMs need (why the format is shaped this way)</a></h2>
<p>Humans <em>browse and recognize</em>; LLMs <em>query (grep/semantic) and follow pointers</em>. So:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Query-shaped keys.</strong> <code>answers:</code> are phrased as the questions you’d actually grep —
not topics a human would skim. Include synonyms and the literal error string.</li>
<li><strong>Canonical vocabulary.</strong> One canonical term per concept; list aliases in <code>answers:</code> so
any search term routes to the one home. (Grep is lexical — drift loses half the hits.)</li>
<li><strong>Atomic, titled units.</strong> One fact per file; the <code>title</code> <em>is</em> the index entry. (A fact
buried at line 480 of a 600-line chapter is grep-findable but expensive to read.)</li>
<li><strong>Self-dating + self-verifying.</strong> <code>date</code> + <code>evidence</code> + <code>reverify</code> let an agent <em>trust</em>
a fact, or re-confirm with <strong>one</strong> command — instead of re-deriving “to be safe.”</li>
<li><strong>Stable anchors + cross-links.</strong> <code>id</code> is a stable anchor; link related facts with
<code>[[other-id]]</code> so one lookup leads to its neighbors.</li>
<li><strong>Pointer, not copy.</strong> A fact card <em>points to</em> the canonical home (book chapter, code,
decision record). The full story stays there; the card makes it findable.</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h2 id="4-what-to-index--and-what-never-to-convert"><a class="header" href="#4-what-to-index--and-what-never-to-convert">4. What to index — and what NEVER to convert</a></h2>
<p><strong>The KM is additive. You convert nothing.</strong> It is a retrieval layer <em>on top of</em> your
existing docs, not a replacement. Each existing layer keeps its job; the KM points into it.</p>
<div class="table-wrapper">
<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Layer</th><th>Role</th><th>KM relationship</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>mdBook</td><td>narrative / teaching</td><td><strong>destination</strong> — a card’s body links to the chapter</td></tr>
<tr><td>live status / changelog</td><td>current state</td><td>not indexed — changes too fast (it’s not a durable fact)</td></tr>
<tr><td>task-trees</td><td>work frontier (layer B)</td><td>not indexed — a fact that <em>emerges</em> from a task is promoted to a card on conclusion</td></tr>
<tr><td>decision records (layer C)</td><td>durable ADR facts</td><td><strong>optionally</strong> add <code>answers:</code> front-matter in place to fold one into the map (zero duplication)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Knowledge Map</strong></td><td>retrieval index</td><td><strong>derived</strong> from the cards above</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
<p><strong>DO</strong> write a card when:</p>
<ul>
<li>you establish a <strong>durable, structural/causal fact</strong> (a root cause, an architectural
constraint, a gotcha, where-something-lives) — i.e. the kind that would otherwise need
archaeology; or</li>
<li>you <strong>catch yourself re-deriving</strong> a fact that should already have been written down
(write the card <em>then</em>, so it never recurs).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>DON’T</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>❌ Convert prose docs/the book/task-trees into cards (destroys narrative, duplicates
content, goes stale — the §12 anti-pattern).</li>
<li>❌ Hand-edit the generated map. It is derived.</li>
<li>❌ Card a <strong>measurement that changes every run</strong> (a metric, today’s count). Card the
durable <strong>conclusion</strong>, not the reading.</li>
<li>❌ Card narrative/teaching (book’s job), current status (live-docs’ job), history
(git’s job).</li>
<li>❌ Duplicate a fact across cards. One canonical card per fact; <code>supersedes</code> when it
changes (don’t silently rewrite).</li>
<li>❌ Omit <code>evidence</code>/<code>reverify</code> — without them an agent re-derives anyway, defeating the
purpose.</li>
<li>❌ Put nondeterminism (timestamps) into the map — it breaks the sync gate.</li>
</ul>
<p>There is <strong>no migration project.</strong> The map grows one cheap card at a time; each card
permanently retires one future archaeology.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="5-the-scripts-and-bounded-projection"><a class="header" href="#5-the-scripts-and-bounded-projection">5. The scripts and bounded projection</a></h2>
<p>The shell entrypoints use one dependency-free Perl core (Perl 5 core modules
only, on BSD/macOS and GNU/Linux):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><code>scripts/gen_knowledge_map.sh</code></strong> scans <code>KM_SCAN_DIRS</code> and writes a small
<code>KM_OUTPUT</code> landing page plus deterministic topic shards under
<code>KM_SHARD_DIR</code>. A topic is the first two kebab-case fact-ID components. Each
fact appears in one topic catalog; each unique question appears in one shard
and may point to multiple facts when the source cards deliberately share it.</li>
<li><strong><code>scripts/query_knowledge_map.sh</code></strong> performs a case-insensitive fixed-
substring search over question rows. Its default cache lives beneath
<code>KM_QUERY_CACHE_DIR</code>; <code>--no-cache</code> reads the committed shards directly and
<code>--all</code> returns every question row. Cache data is disposable and never owns a
fact.</li>
<li><strong><code>scripts/check_knowledge_map.sh</code></strong> rejects missing fields, duplicate IDs,
oversized cards/root/shards/aggregate, missing/extra/stale shards, missing or
duplicated projected facts/questions, and cached/direct result drift.</li>
</ul>
<p><code>gen_knowledge_map.sh --print-map-path</code>, <code>--print-shard-dir</code>, and
<code>--print-generated-paths</code> expose hook-safe paths. Config precedence
(<code>scripts/knowledge_map.conf</code>, all <code>:=</code> assignments) is <strong>environment &gt;
repo-root <code>.knowledge_map.conf</code> &gt; bundle default</strong>. Main knobs are
<code>KM_SCAN_DIRS</code>, <code>KM_OUTPUT</code>, <code>KM_SHARD_DIR</code>, <code>KM_QUERY_CACHE_DIR</code>, and
<code>KM_TITLE</code>; finite card/projection caps also have <code>KM_*_MAX_*</code> overrides for
fixture testing and deliberate adoption calibration.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="6-enforcement--so-the-map-cannot-rot"><a class="header" href="#6-enforcement--so-the-map-cannot-rot">6. Enforcement — so the map cannot rot</a></h2>
<p>Mirror the four-layer defense from <code>MEMORY_ARCHITECTURE.md</code> §9 (a rule nothing checks is a
rule nothing follows):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pre-commit hook</strong> regenerates the root and shards, stages both paths with
deletion awareness, then runs the check — so the agent spends <strong>zero</strong> time
indexing and the projection is always in sync. See <code>hooks/pre-commit.snippet</code>.</li>
<li><strong>CI</strong> runs <code>check_knowledge_map.sh</code> (same script) — catches a bypassed hook; a stale map
or invalid fact <strong>fails the build</strong>. See <code>ci/knowledge-map-gate.yml</code>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Because generation is deterministic, the hook’s “regenerate + stage” makes drift
structurally impossible: the committed map always equals what the facts produce.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="7-read-path--how-a-future-agent-uses-it-no-archaeology"><a class="header" href="#7-read-path--how-a-future-agent-uses-it-no-archaeology">7. Read path — how a future agent uses it (no archaeology)</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>From the bootstrap entrypoint, run
<code>knowledge-map/scripts/query_knowledge_map.sh 'question words'</code>.</li>
<li>Follow the returned repository-relative canonical card path. Use
<code>KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md</code> to browse topic counts or a whole shard when needed.</li>
<li>Trust the fact (it is dated + evidenced), or run its one <code>reverify</code> command.</li>
<li>Only if the fact is genuinely absent is new investigation warranted — and
its conclusion becomes a new card before the turn ends.</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h2 id="8-adoption-checklist-any-repo-already-using-task-trees--memory_architecture"><a class="header" href="#8-adoption-checklist-any-repo-already-using-task-trees--memory_architecture">8. Adoption checklist (any repo already using task-trees + MEMORY_ARCHITECTURE)</a></h2>
<ol>
<li>Copy the <code>knowledge-map/</code> bundle into the repo root; commit it.</li>
<li><code>bash knowledge-map/install.sh</code> (creates <code>docs/knowledge/</code>, generates the first map,
prints the hook + CI wiring) — or do those steps by hand from §6.</li>
<li>Add the hook snippet (<code>hooks/pre-commit.snippet</code>) to <code>.githooks/pre-commit</code>; ensure
<code>git config core.hooksPath .githooks</code> is set.</li>
<li>Add the CI step (<code>ci/knowledge-map-gate.yml</code>) to your pipeline.</li>
<li>Seed it: write a card for each fact you have recently had to re-derive. Going forward,
add a card whenever you establish a durable fact or catch archaeology.</li>
<li><em>(Optional)</em> fold high-traffic <code>docs/decisions/</code> records into the map by adding
<code>answers:</code> front-matter in place.</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h2 id="9-anti-patterns-quick-reference"><a class="header" href="#9-anti-patterns-quick-reference">9. Anti-patterns (quick reference)</a></h2>
<ul>
<li>❌ Hand-editing the generated map.</li>
<li>❌ A big-bang “convert all docs to the KM” project.</li>
<li>❌ Letting a root map, topic shard, individual card, or aggregate grow without
an independent finite bound.</li>
<li>❌ Cards that duplicate book/prose instead of pointing to it.</li>
<li>❌ Carding volatile metrics instead of durable conclusions.</li>
<li>❌ Cards with no <code>evidence</code>/<code>reverify</code> (agent re-derives anyway).</li>
<li>❌ Nondeterministic map output (breaks the sync gate).</li>
<li>❌ Treating the projection or disposable query cache as load-bearing — facts
must stay greppable without either.</li>
<li>❌ An index with no enforcement — it rots.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<p><em>This bundle is itself an instance of what it describes: small, structured, in-repo,
git-tracked, self-describing, and backed by a derive-and-diff gate so it is hard to let it
drift. It composes with — and does not replace — <code>MEMORY_ARCHITECTURE.md</code>.</em></p>
