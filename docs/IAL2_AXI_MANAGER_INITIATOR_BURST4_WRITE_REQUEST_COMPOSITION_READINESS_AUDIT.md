# IAL2 AXI manager initiator — fixed-four write-request composition readiness audit

Owner: `IAL2-AXI-MANAGER-INITIATOR-FRONTIER.44` (behavior-neutral audit).

Date: 2026-07-23

Status: ready for exact public-contract selection. This leaf changes no parser,
generator, public source, support entry, manifest, test, generated artifact,
runtime behavior, or HDL behavior.

## Outcome

The selected fixed-four AXI4 manager AW+W request boundary is implementable as
one additive flat three-child C4 composition:

- the shipped `axi_aw_driver`, reused unchanged;
- the shipped `axi_w_burst4_driver`, reused unchanged; and
- one new zero-state, rule-only
  `axi_write_burst4_request_coordinator`.

One legal command atomically supplies address32, ID4, and four explicit
data32/strobe4 tuples. The top fixes `AWLEN=8'd3`, `AWSIZE=3'd2`, and
`AWBURST=2'b01` (INCR). Admission requires four-byte alignment and a complete
16-byte span within one 4-KiB region. The coordinator starts both children
once, remembers either completion while the other remains outstanding, and
pulses aggregate request done only after the AW transfer and fourth W transfer
have both completed.

The W child's accepted-beat event and two-bit index remain public direct child
outputs. They are not delayed or regenerated by the join coordinator. Request
done is deliberately independent of that per-beat stream: when W finishes
first it waits for AW; when AW finishes first it follows the W child's later
final completion. It does not mean B acceptance or write-transaction success.

A scratch candidate passes strict check, schedule inspection, semantic C4
inspection, Verilator, Yosys, and assertion-disabled plus assertion-enabled
executable proof. It is ready for `.45`, the behavior-neutral exact contract
selector. `.46` is the atomic implementation owner.

## Exact fixed-four legality and assertion-safe spelling

The structural top supplies exact sized constants to the unchanged AW child:

```text
=8'd3  -> aw_driver.cmd_awlen
=3'd2  -> aw_driver.cmd_awsize
=2'b01 -> aw_driver.cmd_awburst
```

Four 32-bit words occupy 16 bytes. A legal address is four-byte aligned and
has low 12 bits no greater than `12'hff0`. The exact coordinator guard and
assertion consequent must use this renderer-safe form:

```text
(& (! cmd_awaddr[0])
   (! cmd_awaddr[1])
   (! (& cmd_awaddr[11] cmd_awaddr[10] cmd_awaddr[9]
         cmd_awaddr[8]  cmd_awaddr[7]  cmd_awaddr[6]
         cmd_awaddr[5]  cmd_awaddr[4]  cmd_awaddr[3]))
   (! (& cmd_awaddr[11] cmd_awaddr[10] cmd_awaddr[9]
         cmd_awaddr[8]  cmd_awaddr[7]  cmd_awaddr[6]
         cmd_awaddr[5]  cmd_awaddr[4]  cmd_awaddr[2])))
```

For aligned addresses, the two final negated products reject exactly the
cases where high low-12 bits plus bit 3 or bit 2 would place the start above
`ff0`. Exhaustive enumeration of all 4,096 low-address values proves equality
with `aligned && low12 <= 12'hff0`. The form accepts legal `...0004` and
`...0ff0`, rejects aligned `...0ff4`, `...0ff8`, and `...0ffc`, strict-checks,
and renders a correct concurrent assertion.

The superficially shorter shipped read-side spelling embeds
`(| bit3 bit2)` under a long `&`. Behavioral lowering preserves it through an
intermediate, but the current concurrent-assertion inlining path loses the
parentheses. That pre-existing defect makes the read assertion reject legal
addresses with bit 2 high even though read admission is correct. This audit
therefore does not copy that textual spelling. The general repair is durably
owned by proposed tree `ISF-ASSERT-NESTED-BITWISE-PRECEDENCE-REPAIR`; this AXI
leaf neither pivots to nor fixes that separate behavior.

## Unchanged children and private bindings

The AW child contract has private `aw_cmd_valid_i`, `aw_cmd_addr_i`, and
`aw_cmd_id_i` inputs plus private `aw_busy_i`/`aw_done_i` outputs. Its
AWREADY/public AW channel bindings and state/rule behavior are unchanged. The
three metadata inputs receive the structural literals above.

The W child contract has private `w_cmd_valid_i`, four `w_cmd_dataN_i`, four
`w_cmd_strbN_i`, and private `w_busy_i`/`w_done_i`. WREADY and WVALID/WDATA/
WSTRB/WLAST remain public. `write_beat_done` and `write_beat_index[1:0]` bind
directly to the unchanged child outputs, preserving their accepted-event
timing exactly.

The coordinator alone captures the aggregate address, ID, data, and strobes,
then presents stable private outputs to the children. A caller may change all
command inputs after the admitted edge without corrupting either child.

## Coordinator schedule and lifecycle

The tested coordinator has 29 interface ports: fifteen inputs and fourteen
outputs. Its lowered actor has 57 signals, zero procedural states, and six
rules with assignment counts `16/2/1/1/5/1`:

1. `admit` checks aggregate/child idle plus legality, captures the complete
   command, clears completion history, raises busy, and pulses both starts;
2. `clear_child_starts` lowers both one-cycle starts;
3. `latch_aw` remembers an observed AW completion;
4. `latch_w` remembers an observed final W completion;
5. `finish_join` retires when current or remembered AW and W completion are
   both true, clears ownership/history/busy, and raises request done; and
6. `clear_done` lowers the request-done event.

Seven explicit priority declarations lower to five realized conflict
resolutions:

- `admit` over `clear_child_starts` for each child-start output;
- `finish_join` over `latch_aw` for `aw_seen_q`;
- `finish_join` over `latch_w` for `w_seen_q`; and
- `finish_join` over `clear_done` for `write_done`.

The two same-value zeroing fan-ins for AW/W completion history are compatible,
not conflicts. The legality assertion uses the exact guard above. No new
counter belongs in the coordinator; the W child remains the only beat
progression authority.

Admission requires `!active_q`, both children not busy, command high, and a
legal address. A command while active is ignored, not queued. AW and W may
complete simultaneously or in either order. Asynchronous active-low reset
aborts coordinator and both children at any phase without a synthetic beat or
request completion; a later legal command restarts cleanly.

PPIF ownership needs an additive import/dispatch arm, root accumulator/object
parser, exact cardinality and mixing checks, predicate, missing-intent entry,
and family-specific `.axi` rejection. Rejection must occur before partial
artifacts escape for:

- wrong root/profile/role/reset;
- missing, duplicate, extra, or reordered-width command/AW/W/status clauses;
- any wrong address/ID/data/strobe/AW metadata/beat-index width;
- authored LEN/SIZE/BURST or beat count, packed/streaming payload, or nested
  child/top override;
- missing beat event/index, busy, or request done;
- duplicate public/private/reserved names or artifact collisions;
- B response bindings or transaction-success claims;
- mixing with standalone AW/W/W-burst4/B/AR/R, any existing composition,
  capacity/status, Valid-Ready, AHB, or APB intent;
- multiple burst4 request objects; or
- `.axi` profile-alias use.

Runtime illegal-address handling is not a replacement for static diagnostics.
With assertions disabled, an illegal idle command launches neither child;
with assertions enabled, it is also a visible caller violation.
